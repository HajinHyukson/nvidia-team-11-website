RECOMMENDATION 02 / FEEDBACK CULTURE02 — 03
02

CHALLENGE THE PROPOSAL.
NOT THE PROPOSER.

AS IS

DIRECT, PUBLIC CRITICISM

NVIDIA's direct feedback culture strengthens debate, but criticism aimed at individuals can create psychological safety concerns.

→

TO BE

KEEP IDEAS PUBLIC, PERSONAL FEEDBACK PRIVATE

Preserve public technical debate, reasoning, and lessons learned. Keep person-focused criticism in smaller settings and address the proposal rather than the person.

EXPECTED RESULT ↗

Higher psychological safety while preserving candid, high-quality debate.

Adapted from Team 11 presentation, slide 20.

RECOMMENDATION 03 / DECISION RIGHTS03 — 03
03

PUT THE PROBLEM
IN RESEARCHERS' HANDS.

AS IS

FOUNDER-CENTERED DECISIONS

Jensen Huang is personally involved in reviewing, challenging, and influencing many technical decisions.

→

TO BE

PROBLEM-OWNER TEAMS

Give researchers ownership of specific technical problems and organize cross-functional collaborators around the problem owner, building on practices illustrated by CUDA-related research.

EXPECTED RESULT ↗

Greater researcher autonomy and less dependence on senior leaders for technical decisions.
